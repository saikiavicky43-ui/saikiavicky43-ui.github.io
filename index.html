Aji kolomtu haatot loi bohisu, Othoso xobdoboro aji bor osinaki. Ki likhu baru ene eta kobita, Jar protitu xarit tair hridoy kopi uthe, Aru tai ebar ubhoti saboloi badhyo hoy. Mor nirobotaro je ek bhaxa ase, Seya tai ketiyau porhi nasale. Kiman je obhiman bukut puhi rakhisu, Seya kunu nikhai hisap koribo nuarile. Moi kunu notun kahini bisora nai, Puroni odhyaytuhe akou ebar melibo bisaru. Jot xomoye amak prithok kora nasil, Jot tair hahitei mor prithibikhon asil. Etiya mathu etai prarthona— Jodi ketiyaba mor ei kobita tair sokut pore, Tente xobdobor majot lukai thoka manuhjonok sini pabo... Jijone ajio tair ubhoti ohar axat Aakou adhorua kobita likhi ase. — VICKY SAIKIA · 30 OCT 2026 About The Author Vicky Saikia A lover of words, poetry and emotions.
